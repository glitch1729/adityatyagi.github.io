Research

M.Sc thesis — Homology groups & homotopy invariants

Advised by Prof. Sanjay Amrutiya, IIT Gandhinagar

Independent study on homology groups of spaces such as the torus and Möbius strip, and the relationship between homotopy and homology — covering the homotopy invariance theorem, long exact sequences, and Mayer–Vietoris, with applications including Brouwer's fixed point theorem.

Read the full thesis (PDF) →

Ongoing — Quasi-cyclic codes over ℤ₄

With Prof. Noah Aydin, Kenyon College

A local-to-global criterion for when 1-generator quasi-cyclic codes over ℤ₄ preserve their seed's module type. Manuscript in preparation for journal submission.

HomotopyContinuationInterface

Macaulay2 package · suggested by Paul Breiding, Universität Osnabrück

Interfaces Macaulay2 with HomotopyContinuation.jl for numerically solving polynomial systems, in the style of the Bertini and PHCpack interfaces.

OnePointAGCodes

Macaulay2 package

Constructs one-point algebraic geometry (Goppa) codes on arbitrary smooth curves via generic Riemann–Roch machinery, validated on Hermitian and Klein quartic curves.
